# Video Lengths and Workflow Fit

Use this reference when Lord Judah asks how long a future video can be, or when choosing between avatar video, image-to-video, and Studio-style workflows.

## Practical Guidance

Short tests in the **5 to 10 second** range are safe and efficient for motion experiments, style proofs, and prompt validation. Videos in the **15 to 45 second** range are suitable for most short-form promos, social clips, and announcements. Videos in the **30 to 90 second** range are usually appropriate for concise talking-head or avatar-led messaging.

The important distinction is that not every workflow has the same limit. **Avatar IV photo-to-video** has a hard cap of **3 minutes**. **Studio-style Avatar IV workflows** can go longer than 180 seconds overall, but a single scene or script box must stay under **180 seconds**, which means longer videos should be broken into multiple scenes. HeyGen pricing and help materials also indicate that some supported plan and workflow combinations can reach **30 minutes** or more for avatar videos, so longer productions are possible when the workflow is selected correctly.

## Decision Table

| Need | Recommended path |
|---|---|
| Fast proof of concept | 5 to 10 second test |
| Short social video | 15 to 45 seconds |
| Speaking promo or announcement | 30 to 90 seconds |
| Multi-minute avatar delivery from a single photo | Use photo-to-video only if under 3 minutes |
| Multi-minute structured presentation | Use Studio-style multi-scene workflow |

## Caution

Do not promise a long-form result until the chosen workflow is confirmed. A request that says only **make a video** is not specific enough to determine the maximum length. Confirm whether the user wants avatar video, image-to-video, uploaded-photo animation, or a multi-scene studio build before committing to duration.
