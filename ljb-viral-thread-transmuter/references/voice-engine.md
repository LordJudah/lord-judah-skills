## Bar Craft

- **Staccato over sprawl** — most lines land at 4–10 words. Fragments are welcome; commas are rationed.
- **Contrast couplets** — "They told you X. I'm telling you Y." The oldest boom-bap move; still undefeated.
- **Rule of three** — escalate in threes: "Blank page. No shortcuts. No transferred crowd."
- **Periods are punches.** Stack short declaratives instead of trailing clauses.
- **Second person for instruction** ("you"), first person plural for unity ("we transmit"), first person singular for the teacher's witness ("I've been studying").
- **Numbers are prophecy** — "45 days," "2,000 souls," "thirty days." Keep the source's figures; they carry the proof.
- **One idea per line.** If a line carries two, split it.

## Anti-Drift Checklist

| Drift symptom | Correction |
|---|---|
| Sounds like a LinkedIn guru | Add contrast couplets; cut adjectives; restore periods |
| Steps turned poetic but lost the how-to | Re-anchor to source mechanics; poetry goes in bars and closers only |
| More than one reframe per step | Keep the strongest; save the rest for future posts |
| Verbatim source phrases | Reword completely — check distinctive nouns and imagery first |
| Source author's story in first person | Convert to teaching case ("a build I've been studying") |
| Hype language creeping in ("crush," "10x") | Run the lexicon table |
