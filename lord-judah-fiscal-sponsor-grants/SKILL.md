# Lord Judah Fiscal Sponsor Grant Workflow

This skill provides the standard operating procedure for Lord Judah / Judah Bless Ent to apply for grants that require 501(c)(3) nonprofit status, utilizing WESPAC Foundation as the fiscal sponsor.

## 1. Overview & Context

- **Applicant:** Cornell "Lord Judah" Carelock (Judah Bless Ent / True He(ART) Academy)
- **Fiscal Sponsor:** WESPAC Foundation (Executive Director: Nada Khader)
- **Primary Programs:** True He(ART) Academy, Hip-Hop for Healing, Creative Education
- **Target Population:** Under-resourced youth, ALICE populations, Black/Latino/immigrant youth in Westchester County (Yonkers, Mount Vernon, White Plains, New Rochelle).

## 2. Grant Search Criteria

When searching for new grants for Lord Judah, prioritize:
- **Focus Areas:** Arts education, youth empowerment, mental health/healing through the arts, social justice, community building.
- **Geographic Scope:** Westchester County, NY, Hudson Valley, or broader NY State/National grants that fund local community work.
- **Eligibility:** Must allow applications from 501(c)(3) organizations or specifically mention fiscal sponsorship.

## 3. Application Workflow

When applying for a grant using WESPAC as a fiscal sponsor, follow these exact steps:

### Step 1: Draft the Grant Narrative & Budget
Lord Judah handles **all** of the heavy lifting. Draft the full grant narrative and program budget before contacting WESPAC.
- **Applicant Name:** Must be listed as "WESPAC Foundation (Fiscal Sponsor for True He(ART) Academy / Judah Bless Ent)"
- **Contact Info:** Use WESPAC's address (255 Grove Street, White Plains, NY 10601) and Nada Khader's email (wespacfoundation@gmail.com).
- **Program Details:** Focus on the impact of True He(ART) Academy (hip-hop, digital arts, mindfulness, trauma-informed education).

### Step 2: Create a Board-Ready Proposal
WESPAC's Board of Directors must approve the fiscal sponsorship for each new grant. Create a clean, professional PDF proposal summarizing:
- The grant opportunity and deadline.
- The program vision and alignment with WESPAC's mission (social justice, equity, community empowerment).
- The target population and geographic focus.
- The full budget breakdown.
- Clear assurance that Lord Judah is managing all application writing, program execution, and reporting.

### Step 3: Email WESPAC (Nada Khader)
Send an email to Nada Khader (`wespacfoundation@gmail.com`) with the Board Proposal attached.
- Request their approval to act as fiscal sponsor for the specific grant.
- State the deadline clearly.
- Request the required compliance documents (see Step 4).
- Note: Nada may call Lord Judah directly to discuss. Prompt Lord Judah to call her at 914-843-0874 if she replies asking for a call.

### Step 4: Collect Compliance Documents
WESPAC will provide the following standard compliance documents. Ensure you have all of them before submitting:
1. 501(c)(3) Tax Exempt Status Certificate (IRS Letter of Determination)
2. Most recent 990 Tax filing or Audited Financial Statement
3. Board of Directors Roster (Current members: Steve Rabinowitz [Chair], Laetitia Berrier Saarbach [Treasurer], Dardan Besilica, Gayle Dunkelberger, Howard Horowitz, Harriet Lowell, Patrick Schelle)
4. WESPAC Organizational Budget

### Step 5: Submit the Application
Compile the grant narrative, program budget, and all WESPAC compliance documents.
- Submit via the required method (online portal, email, etc.).
- If submitting via an online portal, always send a backup copy of the full package via email to the grant administrator if an email address is provided.

### Step 6: Send Thank You Email
Immediately send a thank-you email to Nada Khader confirming that the application has been successfully submitted on WESPAC's behalf.
