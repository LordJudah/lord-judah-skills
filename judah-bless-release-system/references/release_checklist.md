PHASE 1: PRE-RELEASE (T-21 to T-1 Days)

1. T-21 — Finalize Master Recording & Metadata [MANUAL]
  • Confirm final mix/master files (WAV 24-bit/44.1kHz + MP3 320kbps)
  • Lock metadata: title, artist name, featured artists, producer credits, ISRC codes, explicit flag, genre tags
  • Tools: DAW, DistroKid/TuneCore dashboard, metadata spreadsheet

2. T-21 — Register with BMI [MANUAL]
  • Log into BMI account, register work(s) with full writer/publisher splits
  • Confirm ISWC assignment
  • Tools: BMI.com

3. T-18 — Create Cover Art Suite [MANUAL → CAN AUTOMATE VARIANTS]
  • Design primary cover (3000x3000px, RGB, JPG/PNG)
  • Generate platform variants: Spotify Canvas (9:16 vertical video), Apple Music motion art, social square (1080x1080), story format (1080x1920)
  • Tools: Photoshop/Canva, image_generate for AI variants

4. T-14 — Set Up Distribution [MANUAL]
  • Upload to distributor (DistroKid/TuneCore/CD Baby), set release date (T+0), select all stores (Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, Amazon, YouTube Music, Deezer, etc.)
  • Enable pre-save/pre-add campaigns
  • Tools: Distributor dashboard

5. T-14 — Build Shopify Product [MANUAL → CAN AUTOMATE]
  • Create product: digital single/album, vinyl/CD (if applicable), merch bundles
  • Set pricing, inventory, digital delivery automation
  • Design product images using cover art variants
  • Tools: Shopify admin, digital downloads app

6. T-10 — Write Caption & Copy Bank [MANUAL]
  • Draft 10+ caption variations (announcement, behind-the-scenes, lyric quotes, story-driven, call-to-action)
  • Write email newsletter copy (announcement + follow-up)
  • Draft press release (if applicable)
  • Tools: Google Docs, brand voice guide

7. T-7 — Create Social Content Assets [MANUAL → CAN AUTOMATE]
  • Produce 3-5 reels/TikToks (15-60 sec): snippet previews, behind-the-scenes, lyric visualizers, cover art animation
  • Design static posts: cover reveal, tracklist (if project), countdown graphics
  • Tools: CapCut/Premiere, image_generate, video_generate

8. T-7 — Schedule Social Media (Blotato) [AUTOMATED]
  • Queue posts across Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube Community, Threads
  • Schedule: cover reveal (T-7), snippet 1 (T-5), snippet 2 (T-3), countdown (T-1), release day (T-0), post-release (T+1, T+3, T+7)
  • Tools: Blotato dashboard

9. T-5 — Email List Setup (Mailchimp) [AUTOMATED]
  • Create campaign: announcement email (send T-0 at 12:01 AM)
  • Create automation: follow-up email (T+3) with streaming links + Shopify link
  • Segment: full list or engaged subscribers only
  • Tools: Mailchimp

10. T-3 — Pre-Save Campaign Push [AUTOMATED]
  • Activate pre-save link promotion across all platforms
  • Stories, bio links, pinned posts
  • Tools: DistroKid HyperFollow, Feature.fm, or ToneDen

11. T-1 — Final QC & Asset Check [MANUAL]
  • Verify all scheduled posts are queued correctly
  • Confirm distribution is live (check distributor dashboard)
  • Test Shopify purchase flow
  • Confirm email is scheduled
  • Tools: All dashboards

PHASE 2: RELEASE DAY (T-0)

12. T-0 12:01 AM — Release Goes Live [AUTOMATED]
  • Distribution delivers to all platforms
  • Email campaign sends (Mailchimp automation)
  • Tools: Distributor, Mailchimp

13. T-0 9:00 AM — Announcement Post [AUTOMATED via Blotato]
  • Publish cover art + streaming links across all platforms
  • Pin to profiles
  • Tools: Blotato

14. T-0 12:00 PM — Reel/TikTok Drop [AUTOMATED via Blotato]
  • Post primary video content (snippet or visualizer)
  • Tools: Blotato

15. T-0 3:00 PM — Story Blitz [MANUAL → CAN AUTOMATE]
  • Share streaming links, fan reactions, behind-the-scenes clips to Instagram/Facebook stories
  • Tools: Instagram/Facebook native apps, or Blotato story scheduling

16. T-0 6:00 PM — Email Follow-Up [AUTOMATED]
  • Send second email: "Out Now" with direct streaming links + Shopify product link
  • Tools: Mailchimp

17. T-0 Evening — Engage & Amplify [MANUAL]
  • Respond to comments, repost fan stories, share early reactions
  • Tools: Native social apps

PHASE 3: POST-RELEASE (T+1 to T+30)

18. T+1 — Thank You Post [AUTOMATED via Blotato]
  • Gratitude post with streaming link + fan engagement CTA
  • Tools: Blotato

19. T+3 — Lyric Breakdown Content [AUTOMATED via Blotato]
  • Post lyric quotes, bar breakdowns, or behind-the-lyrics content
  • Tools: Blotato

20. T+7 — Performance Metrics Review [MANUAL]
  • Check streaming numbers (Spotify for Artists, Apple Music for Artists, etc.)
  • Review social engagement (Blotato analytics)
  • Check Shopify sales
  • Tools: Platform dashboards

21. T+7 — Playlist Pitching [MANUAL]
  • Submit to Spotify editorial playlists (Spotify for Artists)
  • Reach out to independent playlist curators
  • Tools: Spotify for Artists, SubmitHub, Groover

22. T+10 — User-Generated Content Push [AUTOMATED via Blotato]
  • Encourage fans to create content with the song (dance, lip-sync, covers)
  • Repost best UGC
  • Tools: Blotato

23. T+14 — Second Wave Content [AUTOMATED via Blotato]
  • Drop alternate content: acoustic version, remix, live performance, or music video (if applicable)
  • Tools: Blotato

24. T+14 — Email Recap [AUTOMATED]
  • Send thank-you email with streaming milestones, fan highlights, and Shopify link
  • Tools: Mailchimp

25. T+21 — Long-Tail Content [AUTOMATED via Blotato]
  • Post "1 month later" reflection, fan testimonials, or behind-the-scenes documentary clip
  • Tools: Blotato

26. T+30 — Post-Mortem & Archive [MANUAL]
  • Document what worked, what didn't, key metrics
  • Archive all assets (cover art, videos, captions, email copy) in organized folder
  • Update release checklist template with lessons learned
  • Tools: Google Drive/Notion, analytics exports

• Email copywriting (manual writing, automated sending)

SCALABILITY NOTES
• Single: Use all phases as written. Total timeline: 21 days prep + 30 days post-release.
• EP/Album: Extend pre-release to T-30 days. Add tracklist reveal (T-10), single rollout strategy (stagger releases), and album listening party (virtual or in-person) at T-0.
• Deluxe/Re-Release: Compress pre-release to T-7 days. Focus on new content (bonus tracks, remixes) and re-engage existing audience.
