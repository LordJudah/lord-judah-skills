# The Rap Dojo: 26-Day Strategic Content Plan

## I. Strategic Objective
The Rap Dojo is not just a content series; it is a **funnel**. By running alongside Days 25–51 of the Rap Profiles series, The Rap Dojo will:
1.  **Educate:** Break down the craft of MCing using martial arts frameworks.
2.  **Identify:** Segment the audience into specific cohorts (creatives, fans, educators).
3.  **Test Demand:** Determine if the ultimate destination should be a standalone App or a Web-based platform.
4.  **Validate Products:** Test interest in physical/digital merch (shirts, stationery, courses) before full production.

## III. Audience Intelligence Strategy (App vs. Website)
To determine whether The Rap Dojo should live as a native mobile App or a Web platform, we will embed specific "feelers" into the content.

**The Strategy:**
Instead of asking directly, "Should I build an app?", we ask behavioral questions to see how the audience *wants* to interact with the content.

**App Signals (On-the-go, interactive, daily utility):**
*   **Day 28 (Language):** "Would you use a digital rhyme dictionary built for MCs?" (Tests desire for utility tools)
*   **Day 35 (Orange Belt):** "Do you prefer learning via mobile app or desktop courses?" (Direct A/B test)
*   **Day 46 (Metaphor Kata):** "Would daily Kata prompts on your phone help you write?" (Tests push-notification/daily engagement model)
*   *If these get high engagement, the audience wants a utility App.*

**Web Signals (Deep study, courses, community):**
*   **Day 35 (Orange Belt):** Responses favoring desktop/deep-dive formats.
*   **Day 40 (Black Belt):** "If we built a virtual Cypher room, would you join?" (Tests desire for synchronous web-based community/video integration)
*   *If these get high engagement, the audience wants a Web-based academy/portal.*

**Data Collection Mechanic:**
Use ManyChat keyword triggers (e.g., "Comment APP or WEB") on these specific days to auto-segment the audience into ManyChat lists for future retargeting.

## IV. Product Creation Feeler Plan (Merch & Tools)
While the series runs, we will test demand for physical and digital products without committing to full inventory.

**The Strategy:**
Use the "Assume the Sale" approach. Mention a hypothetical product related to the day's lesson and gauge the reaction in the comments.

**Product Categories to Test:**
1.  **Stationery/Tools:**
    *   *The Rap Dojo Practice Journal* (Tested on Day 43 - Rhyme Kata)
    *   *The 'Content is Mind' Notebook* (Tested on Day 30 - Content Discipline)
2.  **Apparel (Streetwear meets Conscious Branding):**
    *   *Belt Rank Hoodies* (e.g., "Green Belt Writer" - Tested on Day 36)
    *   *Kata Graphic Tees* (e.g., "Cipher Kata" - Tested on Day 48)

**Execution Pipeline:**
1.  Post the feeler CTA.
2.  If engagement (comments/likes) spikes above the baseline average for the series, the signal is validated.
3.  The agent can immediately use the Printify/Shopify integration to generate the mockup for the validated item and push the product live *while the series is still running*, capturing immediate intent and capitalizing on the hype.
